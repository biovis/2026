**Talk Title: Traceability in the Land of Wicked Problems**
Jennifer Rogers, Idaho National Laboratory
 

Abstract: What do a design study and data governance have in common? More than you would think. This talk traces a research journey in traceability, beginning with design-oriented visualization research, where traceability emerged as a way to understand decisions and rationale in unreproducible work. It then extends to human-in-the-loop systems, where transparent interaction between people, models, and data is essential, and finally to governance in large-scale, collaborative data ecosystems.
