Join us on *Wednesday, 21st of January* at 5pm Berlin / 4 pm
London:
**“Crafting visual biomedical stories“**
Laura Garrison, University of Bergen

Title: Crafting visual biomedical stories   

Abstract: While visualizations are often described as vehicles to efficient insights, they are equally valuable for communicating and instilling wonder in the sciences. In this talk, I will discuss practical considerations for crafting impactful biomedical stories, ranging from illustration to more abstract clinically-oriented visual tools.
